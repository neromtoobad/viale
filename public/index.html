Good to know

Still unsure? The judges’ guide walks through every claim in five minutes.

Verify in 5 minutes →
Is any of this real money?

No. Payments are in EUDemo, a test euro token on Concordium testnet. The checks, signatures, payments and seals are real testnet transactions you can open on the explorer.

Do I need a wallet?

No. Without one, Viale’s demo account signs the mandate and the log says so. With the Concordium Browser Wallet on testnet, your own account signs, and the agent can only do what you signed.

Why did the agent walk away from a Bottle Shop?

The street has a planted fake: an agent named “The Bottle Shop” with a valid badge, owned by an account with only a personal identity. Check 3 fails, so the agent refuses to pay it.

Why does the bottle errand stop?

The real Bottle Shop sells only to adults, so it asks for a zero-knowledge 18+ proof from the Concordium ID app; the shop learns only yes or no. The ID app is a mainnet app and we could not finish a proof on testnet, so the errand waits 2 minutes, then stops with nothing paid. The code for the proof is written and in the repository.

What if the model tries to overspend?

It can’t. The limits are checked in code before every payment, outside the model. A payment over the cap or budget, after expiry, or to a shop you did not allow is refused and logged.

How do I know a log wasn’t edited?

Each log’s SHA-256 hash is registered on chain when the errand ends. Paste a log into the audit desk and it recomputes the hash and compares it with the transaction.

Can my business be on the street?

Yes. Open a shop with your Concordium wallet: you register your own agent, sign your price list, and the errand agent checks you like any other shop.
