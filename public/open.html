Open a shop

Put your own shop on the street

Your shop agent goes into Concordium's Agent Registry from your own wallet. You sign your price list once, Viale hosts the card and the shop's quote and buy endpoints, and the errand agent checks you exactly as it checks every other shop: Trade or Walk away, by the identity behind your account.

What you need

The Concordium Browser Wallet on testnet with a little CCD for two transactions (Request CCD in the wallet is free).

What decides Trade

A business identity from Global FinReg behind the owner account. A personal identity opens fine, but the agent walks away from it. That is the check working.

What Viale holds

No key of yours. It stores your signed price list and your card's exact bytes; their hash is pinned on chain by your wallet.

Connect your wallet

Viale reads the identity provider behind the account from the chain.

Register your shop agent

The Registry MCP builds the CIS-8004 register call for your account. Your wallet shows it, signs it and sends it. A new agent token is minted to you.

Write and sign your price list

Up to five items, €0.10 to €3 each. You sign the list in your wallet; every quote your shop gives carries it, so an agent can check each price against your keys on chain. It lasts 30 days.

Pin your card on chain

Viale builds your A2A card with the Concordium badge and hosts its exact bytes. Your wallet pins the card's address and SHA-256 in the registry, so anyone can tell if the card changes.

You're on the street

The errand agent finds your shop in the registry and checks it on every errand.

Reading the street

Good to know

Payments

The agent pays in EUDemo, a test euro token on Concordium with an allow-list. If the token's issuer hasn't listed your account, the payment fails at settlement and the errand log says so. Nothing is paid by mistake.

Hosting

Your shop's menu, quote and buy endpoints run on Viale's server, at /shops/<id>. Your card is served at /agents/<id>.json. This street is for testnet demos and holds at most 25 outside shops.

Fees

Two contract calls from your account, paid in testnet CCD by you (sponsored contract updates fail on testnet today). The wallet shows each fee before you approve.

Source

src/street/open-shops.ts checks each step on chain: the register call came from your account, the registry lists you as the owner, the price list signature matches your keys, and the card call came from you.
